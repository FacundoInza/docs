The **OTC Desk** turns your WhatsApp number into a crypto trading desk. A **bot** serves your customers: it shows your price, asks for their details and opens the order. You only **check the payment in your bank, confirm it and send the crypto**.

Setup takes **5 steps, about 10 minutes**, from **OTC Desk → Summary**, with the **«Set up your OTC desk»** guide. Each step has a short video (in Spanish).

## What you need

- The **WhatsApp Business** app, up to date, on the number's phone.
- The phone and a **computer** at hand: the connection ends by scanning a code from the app.
- The **Facebook** account that manages your business.

<Warning>
  WhatsApp restricts crypto trading. We recommend connecting a **secondary number**, not your business's main one, in case Meta limits it.
</Warning>

## 1. Connect your WhatsApp

<Frame caption="Step 1 of 6 — Connect your WhatsApp">
  <iframe width="100%" height="400" src="https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/oU2mOpIrUIc" title="OTC Desk 1/6: Connect your WhatsApp" frameborder="0" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture" referrerpolicy="strict-origin-when-cross-origin" allowfullscreen />
</Frame>

1. In **Your WhatsApp**, enter a **Number name** and click **Create and connect**.
2. Meta's page opens: sign in with Facebook, pick or create your business and accept the permissions.
3. Open **WhatsApp Business** on the phone and scan the code shown on screen.
4. Back in Silver5, the number shows as **connected**. You keep using WhatsApp on the phone as usual.

## 2. Where you get paid

<Frame caption="Step 2 of 6 — Where you get paid">
  <iframe width="100%" height="400" src="https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/tTZ-umRfxeo" title="OTC Desk 2/6: Where you get paid" frameborder="0" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture" referrerpolicy="strict-origin-when-cross-origin" allowfullscreen />
</Frame>

The account where customers pay you when you sell. Three fields: **Bank or wallet** (any country), **Holder** and **Account number or alias**. Click **Save account**. When a customer opens an order, the bot sends them these details. To collect in more than one account, add the others from the ad with **Add collection account**.

## 3. Your first ad

<Frame caption="Step 3 of 6 — Your first ad">
  <iframe width="100%" height="400" src="https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/OOzcQURfN4s" title="OTC Desk 3/6: Your first ad" frameborder="0" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture" referrerpolicy="strict-origin-when-cross-origin" allowfullscreen />
</Frame>

The ad is the price, limits and quota the bot sells with. It comes prefilled (sell USDT, TRC20, 30 minutes to pay, paid into your account). You fill in the **currency** (suggested from your WhatsApp number), the **price**, the **minimum and maximum per order** and the **total quota**. **More options** shows the full ad and **How your customer sees it**.

In sell ads, **Customer details** sets what the bot asks before the summary: **full name** (if off, the order uses the customer's WhatsApp profile name) and an **ID document** matching the ad's currency — cédula or RIF (VES), cédula (COP), DNI or CUIT (ARS), RUT (CLP), CPF (BRL), DNI (PEN), ID card (BOB), CURP (MXN), or a generic ID document for other currencies.

From **another phone**, scan the code or open the link and write like a customer: «hola, quiero comprar USDT». The bot answers in seconds; the guide switches to **Your desk is online** with your **customer link and QR code** to share.

## 5. Handle an order

<Frame caption="Step 5 of 6 — Handle an order">
  <iframe width="100%" height="400" src="https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/rdACRTw8IDQ" title="OTC Desk 5/6: Handle an order" frameborder="0" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture" referrerpolicy="strict-origin-when-cross-origin" allowfullscreen />
</Frame>

The customer pays and sends a screenshot; the bot records it and the order moves to **Payment reported**. Open it in **Orders**, **check your bank**, click **I saw the payment in my bank** and confirm. Send the crypto from your wallet (copy the address from the panel, not the chat), then click **Release**, paste the transaction hash and confirm. The bot notifies the customer at every step.

<Warning>
  A receipt can be faked. Only confirm when you see the money credited in your bank.
</Warning>

## 6. Day to day

<Frame caption="Step 6 of 6 — Day to day">
  <iframe width="100%" height="400" src="https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/NgVbwXcLyT4" title="OTC Desk 6/6: Day to day" frameborder="0" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture" referrerpolicy="strict-origin-when-cross-origin" allowfullscreen />
</Frame>

- **Change the price:** in **Ads**, the pencil next to the price → new price → **Save**.
- **Quota:** the bar shows what's left; **Add quota** when it runs low.
- **One ad:** turn off its switch; the others stay online.
- **Turn off the bot:** turns off all online ads; open orders continue. **Turn on the bot** brings the same ads back online.
- **Credits:** each WhatsApp message your desk sends uses **15 credits**. If they run out, the bot stops replying until you top up.
