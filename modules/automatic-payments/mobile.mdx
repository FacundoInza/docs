The **Mobile** variant automates your P2P order payments with the **Silver5 desktop app**, which operates the bank app on an **Android phone** (the **BlueStacks** emulator or a real phone over USB). Silver5 sends you the orders and the app pays them on its own, one by one — and you can have **several phones paying in parallel**.

<Note>
  Would you rather have payments run in the cloud, with nothing to install and no phone to use? Check out the [Browser Automatic Payments](/modules/automatic-payments/browser) variant.
</Note>

## How it works

<Tip>
  That video walks the whole flow in one go. If you'd rather take it one piece at a time, each step has its own short video below, under [Step-by-step setup](#step-by-step-setup).
</Tip>

Each of your **bank accounts** lives on **one phone**. When an order comes in, Silver5 looks at which account the money has to come out of and sends the payment to the phone assigned to that account. The desktop app opens the bank app on that phone and runs the payment step by step, all the way to capturing the receipt.

That's where the three rules of the model come from:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="The account decides" icon="route">
    Which phone a payment runs on is decided by the **bank account**, not by the operator. Nothing you touch in the desktop app changes the routing.
  </Card>

  <Card title="One payment per phone" icon="lock">
    Each phone handles **one payment at a time**. Payments from the same account run in sequence; different phones run in parallel.
  </Card>

  <Card title="One bank per phone" icon="mobile-screen-button">
    Two accounts at the **same bank** need **two phones**: on the same device they overwrite each other's session.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

If the phone assigned to an account is off or disconnected, that payment **does not run on a different one** — it waits. That's by design: a payment run on the wrong phone comes out of the wrong bank account.

### The setup order

```text
BlueStacks → bank app → desktop app → credentials → connect → bank account + phone → test payment → activate
```

Everything involving the desktop app comes first and together; you touch the cloud only once, at the end.

<Warning>
  **Activating is always the last step.** Once you activate the integration, real payments start coming in — so the test payment goes **before** it, never the other way around.
</Warning>

## What you need

- A computer running **Windows or Mac**, with the **Silver5 desktop app** installed.
- **BlueStacks** (or a real Android phone over USB).
- Your bank's app and your login credentials.
- Your Silver5 **connection token**.

<Tip>
  Wondering how many phones your computer can handle and how to configure each BlueStacks instance? It's all in [PC Requirements](/modules/automatic-payments/pc-requirements).
</Tip>

## Step-by-step setup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the BlueStacks instance">
    Open BlueStacks and create an instance. That instance is going to be **your phone**.

    Turn on the debug bridge once: ⚙ **Settings → Advanced → Android Debug Bridge → ON**. Without it, the desktop app can't see the phone.

    <Frame caption="Step 1 — Installing BlueStacks (video in Spanish)">
      <iframe width="100%" height="315" src="https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/WzX5w9hkyac" title="Step 1: Installing BlueStacks" frameborder="0" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture" referrerpolicy="strict-origin-when-cross-origin" allowfullscreen />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install the bank app and log in once">
    Inside the instance, open the **Play Store** and install your bank's app. Log in **once by hand** and leave the session open.

    <Warning>
      Leave the bank app **logged in**. If the session closes, the payment won't go through.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install the Silver5 desktop app">
    In Silver5 → **Settings → Desktop app connection → Download**. Install it and open it.

    <Frame caption="Step 2 — Installing the desktop app (video in Spanish)">
      <iframe width="100%" height="315" src="https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/ytTsvjmWzUo" title="Step 2: Installing the desktop app" frameborder="0" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture" referrerpolicy="strict-origin-when-cross-origin" allowfullscreen />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check that the phone shows up on its own">
    In the app's **📱 Devices** section, your phone appears on its own. There's nothing to enter by hand.

    <Frame caption="Step 3 — Connecting the device (BlueStacks) (video in Spanish)">
      <iframe width="100%" height="315" src="https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/0RqInPs3xJI" title="Step 3: Connecting the device (BlueStacks)" frameborder="0" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture" referrerpolicy="strict-origin-when-cross-origin" allowfullscreen />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Load the bank credentials">
    In the **Credentials** section you'll already see the fields for the bank you installed, grouped **by phone**. Enter the username and password and save.

    <Warning>
      🔒 Credentials are stored **encrypted in your computer's keychain** (Keychain on Mac, Credential Locker on Windows). They **never** travel to Silver5 nor are they stored in plain text. You hold custody.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect the app to Silver5 (token)">
    In Silver5 → **Settings → Desktop app connection → Generate token**. Confirm with your **2FA code**. The token is shown **only once** — copy it right then.

    In the desktop app, paste the token and tap **Connect to Silver5**. At the top it should read **🟢 Connected to Silver5**.

    <Frame caption="Step 4 — Connecting the desktop app to Silver5 (video in Spanish)">
      <iframe width="100%" height="315" src="https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/HuqH81Oc64c" title="Step 4: Connecting the desktop app to Silver5 (cloud)" frameborder="0" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture" referrerpolicy="strict-origin-when-cross-origin" allowfullscreen />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the bank account and assign it the phone">
    In Silver5 → **Bank accounts → New**. Choose the **bank**, the **Mobile** channel, and **assign the operator** who has the desktop app open.

    On the **Device** card, tap **Search** and pick the phone. That's where this account's money will come out of.

    <Frame caption="Step 5 — Creating the bank account (video in Spanish)">
      <iframe width="100%" height="315" src="https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/0td0TP7Z6Dw" title="Step 5: Creating the bank account in Silver5 (cloud)" frameborder="0" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture" referrerpolicy="strict-origin-when-cross-origin" allowfullscreen />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run a test payment">
    Before activating anything, run a test payment: pick the account, a **small amount**, and a **destination of your own**.

    Watch the emulator while it runs — that's exactly what it will do with every order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the integration and set the limits">
    If the test payment went through, go to the bank account and **create the integration**. Choose which **payment method** it's for and set the **limits**.

    <Frame caption="Step 6 — Creating the payment agent (video in Spanish)">
      <iframe width="100%" height="315" src="https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/aOrrXhljQJ0" title="Step 6: Creating the payment agent" frameborder="0" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture" referrerpolicy="strict-origin-when-cross-origin" allowfullscreen />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate">
    Activate the integration. From that moment on, orders are paid automatically, within the limits you set.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Leave the desktop app open and the phone on. That's all.
</Tip>

## Several phones at once

You can have **several bank accounts paying in parallel**, one per phone. To add a phone, you only repeat the emulator part:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a second BlueStacks instance">
    Same as the first one, with its **Android Debug Bridge → ON**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install the other account's bank and log in once">
    Leave the session open, just like on the first one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Load that phone's credentials">
    In **Credentials** you'll now see **two groups, one per phone**: each asks for the credentials of the bank installed on it. Enter the second one's and save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the second bank account and assign it the second phone">
    Same flow as before: **Bank accounts → New** → **Device** card → **Search** → the second phone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create its integration, set limits, and activate">
    And that's it: both phones pay at the same time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  **One bank per phone.** If you have two accounts at the same bank, you need two phones — and **both** must have their phone assigned. If one is left without a phone, both fall into the same lane and the payment runs on whichever device is available: which account the money comes out of becomes a coin flip.
</Warning>

<Note>
  Once you have more than one phone, a **selector** appears at the top of the app to choose which one you're working with. That selector is **working context**: it changes what you see in the manual views (Payments, Test payment, New bank, Credentials) and does **not** change which phone an automatic payment runs on. That's always decided by the bank account.
</Note>

<Info>
  Each phone handles **one payment at a time**. Two phones, twice the payments in parallel.
</Info>

<Note>
  How many phones you can keep running at once depends on your computer's RAM and cores — with 16 GB and 4 cores it's 3. The full table, the settings for each instance, and how to verify it on your machine are in [PC Requirements](/modules/automatic-payments/pc-requirements).
</Note>

## Using a real phone over USB

Instead of the emulator you can connect a real Android phone.

<Accordion title="Steps to connect over USB" icon="usb">
  <Steps>
    <Step title="Connect the phone over USB">
      Plug the phone into the computer with the cable.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Enable Developer options">
      On the phone: **Settings → About phone** and tap **Build number** 7 times until it says "You are now a developer".
    </Step>
    <Step title="Enable USB debugging">
      On the phone: **Settings → Developer options → USB debugging**.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Accept the permission">
      Accept the **"Allow USB debugging?"** prompt that appears on the phone.
    </Step>
  </Steps>

  From there the phone shows up under **📱 Devices** and the rest of the setup is the same.
</Accordion>

## The bank's second factor (2FA)

For banks that require a second factor with a 6-digit code (TOTP), the app can generate that code on its own. You set it up by importing the QR exported from Google Authenticator.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Export the 2FA from Google Authenticator">
    Open Google Authenticator → menu → **Transfer accounts → Export accounts**. Select the bank account and generate the **QR code**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the QR in the Silver5 app">
    In the credentials section, add the 2FA using the **exported QR code**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  🔒 The 2FA secret is stored **encrypted on your computer**, just like the bank credential. It **never** travels to Silver5.
</Warning>

## Executing a payment

When an order comes in, the app **loads and validates** the data against the order, shows a preview, and runs the payment on that account's phone.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Manual confirmation" icon="hand">
    Enable **Manual confirmation** when the bank doesn't allow confirming from outside. The app prepares and validates everything, then stops right before the last step. You (or the customer) do the last step —**Confirm** and your password— **on the phone**; then you go back to the app, tap **I've confirmed**, and the app captures the receipt.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Stop an automation" icon="stop">
    The **⏹ Stop** button cuts off the automation at any time, before sending. The app stops cleanly between steps, without leaving the payment half done.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Note>
  The app **blocks the send** if the data (ID number, phone, bank, or amount) doesn't match the order.
</Note>

## Multiple orders in a row

Silver5 queues the orders and dispatches them **per phone**: each device runs one at a time, in the order they came in, and different phones move forward in parallel. You don't have to do anything: leave the app connected and the phones logged in.

## Common issues

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The phone doesn't show up under Devices">
    Check that **Android Debug Bridge** is ON in BlueStacks (⚙ Settings → Advanced), or USB debugging if it's a real phone. Then reopen the desktop app.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A payment is waiting and says the device is disconnected">
    The phone assigned to that account isn't online. Payments **do not run on a different phone**: turn that device on and the payment continues on its own. It's the safeguard that keeps the money from coming out of the wrong account.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I have two accounts at the same bank and one doesn't pay">
    Both accounts need **their own assigned phone**, each with the bank app installed and its own credentials loaded. Check that neither one was left without a device.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I changed the phone selector and the payment ran on the other one">
    That's the expected behavior. The selector only changes what you see in the manual views; automatic payment routing is decided by the bank account.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="'Silver5 disconnected' in a loop">
    Don't open the app **twice**: only a single instance should run. Close the others and reopen just one.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The bank rejects by location">
    Use **manual confirmation** or connect from a connection in the bank's country.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The payment stopped at 'Confirm'">
    Check the phone: if it asks for a code or password, complete it (or use manual confirmation).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Security

<Warning>
  - The **bank credentials** and the **Silver5 token** live **encrypted on your computer**; they don't leave it. Silver5 never receives your bank username or password.
  - Credentials are stored **per phone**: each device uses its own.
  - A payment **only** runs on the phone assigned to that bank account. When in doubt, it doesn't pay.
  - The **last step of the payment** can always be done or confirmed by you.
  - The app **does not confirm** a payment whose data doesn't match the order.
</Warning>
